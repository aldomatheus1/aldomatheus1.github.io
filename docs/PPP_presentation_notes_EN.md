# PPP oral presentation — Structure for 6 minutes

This is a **preparation outline, not a completed slide deck**.

1. **Target careers and value proposition** — Electronics, embedded systems, or telecommunications. State a tentative target grounded in research.
2. **Self-assessment** — Technical projects, languages, teamwork and leadership; be concrete about strengths and gaps.
3. **Evidence and networking** — Summarize at least three *actual* professional interviews and what they changed in your understanding.
4. **Three Odyssey Plans** — A: electronics/embedded systems; B: telecommunications/signal processing; C: R&D; explain alternative roles.
5. **International mobility roadmap** — Three credible scenarios with distinct target environments, skills needed, and actions.
6. **Action plan and conclusion** — Dates, internships and projects, Plan B, bibliography/credits and questions to jury.

## Required additions
- Real mentor-interview notes and permission to publish, if applicable.
- Verifiable sources for industry analysis and the mobility proposals.
- Updated EN + FR CVs adapted to target positions.
- Real presentation file (6–8 slides), not just this outline.
- 2–3 minute English elevator-pitch video, to be embedded/linked once recorded.
